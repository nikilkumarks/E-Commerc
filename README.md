# 🛍️ E-Commerce Website

A responsive and modern e-commerce frontend built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. This project showcases a variety of pages typical to an online store, including product listings, detailed product views, shopping cart, and more.

## 🌐 Live Demo

Explore the live website: [e-commerc-seven.vercel.app](https://e-commerc-seven.vercel.app)
 ## 📁 Project Structure

## ✨ Features

- **Home Page**: Highlights featured products and promotions.
- **Shop Page**: Displays a range of products with images and prices.
- **Product Detail Page**: Provides detailed information about a selected product.
- **Cart Page**: Allows users to view and manage items in their shopping cart.
- **About & Contact Pages**: Share information about the company and provide contact details.
- **Blog Section**: Contains articles or news related to the store or products.
- **Responsive Design**: Ensures optimal viewing on various devices.
